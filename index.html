BEHIND THE SCENES

The Dad Found Something studio

This website introduces the brand and explains its private production application. Aaron Hart operates the studio to plan, review, assemble, and publish videos for the Dad Found Something accounts. The application is being developed for the owner's use; it is not a public sign-up service.

01 / THE STORY

Start with a real-life moment.

Warm household humor, a coherent setup and payoff, and Dad speaking in his own voice—not a parade of product pitches.

02 / THE REVIEW

Give every scene a purpose.

Scripts, storyboards, and finished videos go through human review. AI-assisted production does not replace the owner's approval.

03 / THE RELEASE

Publish with permission.

A separate publishing approval covers the chosen video, accounts, captions, and disclosures. Connecting an account is not permission to post.

GOOGLE & YOUTUBE CONNECTION

Access for Dad's channel.
Not yours.

The private application uses YouTube API Services. The owner authorizes read-only YouTube access to check channel identity and publication results, plus upload access to send expressly approved videos and their titles, descriptions, and disclosure settings.

The public website does not offer Google login, collect passwords, or connect visitors' accounts. Read our privacy policy for storage, sharing, and revocation details, and our terms for use of this site.
